# Compliance notes (automated outreach)

This tool can **auto-send** replies to inbound leads. Automated email is regulated — a quick,
non-legal-advice checklist so you deploy it responsibly.

## CAN-SPAM (US)

- **Identify yourself.** Accurate "From"/"Reply-To" and a real physical mailing address in the footer.
- **No deceptive subject lines.** The subject must reflect the content.
- **Honor opt-outs** within 10 business days; include a working unsubscribe mechanism.

## GDPR / ePrivacy (EU/UK)

- **Lawful basis.** For B2B inbound where the person contacted you first, *legitimate interest* is
  usually defensible — but document it.
- **Transparency & access.** Be ready to tell a person what you store and to delete it on request.
- **Data minimization.** This service is stateless by default; your CRM is the system of record.

## How the tool helps you stay compliant

- **Inbound-only by design.** It replies to people who contacted *you* — it is not a cold-outreach
  blaster.
- **Confidence gate.** Low-confidence leads are held for human review, not auto-sent.
- **Footer hook.** The draft templates are the place to inject your required identification +
  unsubscribe line before enabling auto-send.

> This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Confirm requirements for your jurisdiction and
> mailing volume.
